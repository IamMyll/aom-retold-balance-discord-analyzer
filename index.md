### Insights into Game Balance

Players frequently highlighted Egyptian frailty as a core issue, with **Teddabear** noting that their problems stem from “death by a thousand cuts” and that the civ needs “a pretty much across the board hit point boost.” Camels drew mixed takes: **Teddabear** felt they “aren’t supposed to be that good because they are so fast,” while **idc** (backagain555) countered that only high-level players like Kimo were raiding correctly with them. Suggestions of adding wood costs to Egyptian buildings (from **v2s28**) reflected a desire to slow their eco/military tempo without gutting identity.

Demeter (and by extension Greek minor-god tools) generated positive experimentation. **Rank Donator** asked about labbing mass wolves in Classical, and **jayyyred** wanted mass Lykaon. **Mathilde Moksha** shared Boit’s forward-hunt Lykaon gameplan—treating the myth units as both army and extra villagers—which **Illuminatio imperii** called a solid (if map-dependent) strat and **idc** viewed as potentially S-tier but “unfun by design,” limiting exploration. **Linger**’s clip of camel raiders melting into Demeter gatherers + Lykaons underscored how strong the defensive/offensive hybrid can feel on hunt-heavy maps.

The loudest and most recent balance conversation centered on Chinese raiding myth units. **Ironhide**, **Mathilde Moksha**, **v2s28**, and **RecoN** described Raiju, Qinglongs, Fei beasts, Tengu, and butterfly units as oppressive: extremely fast (Raiju at 7.3), able to fly/jump walls or forests, hard to spot, resistant to towers, and packing front-loaded specials that delete villager clumps before the attack notification fully registers—especially when multiple Raiju fire simultaneously (which **boit** identified as unintended/bug behavior, since launched units should become unhittable). **Mathilde Moksha** emphasized the qualitative difference versus ordinary cavalry: cav lack AoE specials, cannot ignore terrain, and die far more readily to arrows and buildings. **Ironhide** noted that prior nerfs already hit Tengu, butterflies, Fei beasts, and centaurs for similar reasons, and argued the same treatment (ability damage vs villagers) should apply so the units can be balanced around military instead. Counter-views existed—**Tunison** and **SoloJebachOnly** felt map defense and the units themselves were “fine,” and **boit** found mass cavalry more frustrating in both 1v1 and team games—yet the consensus lean was that the current fantasy-raiding package is unfun even when “beatable.”

Broader themes included the high skill ceiling and micro intensity of myth-unit raids versus more traditional cavalry, the value of defensive buildings being undermined by flying/high-speed myth units, and occasional calls for range standardization (e.g., **Teddabear** wanting certain units at 6 range).

### Recommended Balance Changes

- Grant Egyptians a broad hit-point increase across human units and/or key buildings to reduce “death by a thousand cuts” fragility (**Teddabear**).
- Re-evaluate Camel Rider damage or raid efficiency so their high speed is offset by clearer counter-play or slightly lower kill potential; consider minor wood costs on selected Egyptian buildings if eco power remains too high (**Teddabear**, **v2s28**, **idc**).
- Nerf or redesign Raiju special so multiple units cannot simultaneously dump full damage on the same villager clump (fix the “all specials fire at once” behavior); reduce base speed (currently cited at 7.3) and/or slash ability damage specifically versus villagers (**boit**, **Ironhide**, **v2s28**, **Mathilde Moksha**).
- Apply parallel villager-ability damage reductions (or longer cooldowns / smaller AoE) to Qinglongs, Fei beasts, and remaining high-mobility Chinese raiders so towers and defensive buildings regain value; keep military-versus-military performance intact (**Ironhide**, **v2s28**, **Mathilde Moksha**).
- Standardize or lower the effective range of problematic ranged myth units/abilities to 6 where appropriate (**Teddabear**).
- Monitor Demeter Lykaon/wolf openings for over-performance on hunt maps; small cost or train-time tweaks may be needed if the “extra villager army” pattern becomes ubiquitous, while preserving the creative forward-hunt identity (**Rank Donator**, **Mathilde Moksha**, **boit**, **Illuminatio imperii**).
- General pass on flying/wall-ignoring myth unit pathing and revelation so they cannot as easily appear inside bases with zero warning, improving reaction windows without removing the fantasy fantasy.
