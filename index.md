### Insights into Game Balance
Players widely view Egypt as one of the weakest pantheons across all ELOs, with [.teddabear] noting it performs poorly even at lower levels and [boit] describing its units (barring spears, chariot archers, and champion elephants) as "crap" while eco feels fine but timings lack punch. [benqvh1990] highlighted how China's recent 5% eco buff snowballs into faster TCs and upgrades, exacerbating Egypt's powercreep issues against DLC civs, and [_linger] argued Soul Guides outclass post-nerf Mummies by applying debuffs on every attack for constant summons without the latter's micro-heavy AoE cooldown. Greek balance draws mixed but mostly critical feedback: [farbeyonddriven2000_17994] and [wrrwh] called Demeter unfun and underperforming (with Amazon Archers now worse than Toxotes or even Heroic Chariot Archers per pop/DPS after losing divine damage), while Hippeus lag behind buffed Raiding Cavalry in direct fights despite slower speed, as tested by [wrrwh] and [rankdonator]. Poseidon and Zeus hold up better via myth units and favor, but [eric1075] and [reconrts] see Greeks as C-tier on land due to poor mobility and matchups into Japan/Aztec/China. Aztecs remain polarizing and strong, with [mathilde_moksha_333] noting lingering OP units/interactions at high level (e.g., Warrior Priests as tagless generalists with high HP/multipliers outperforming Hoplites early, per multiple tests), Jaguar Riders still potent post-nerfs, and Tezcatlipoca's Heroic Jags enabling unhealthy FH spikes that [macdoom_] and others want reverted. China feels overtuned via Qinglongs (hard-to-kill flyers that [reconrts], [bigstimulus], and [rankdonator] call the worst-designed myth for raiding/defense), Kuafu eco, and favored land, while Japan’s Bushido and Raiju enable oppressive early-mid aggression that [eric1075] proved nearly unbeatable even with top micro. Atlanteans draw eco complaints ([yungoath1] on Oracle hero costs post-Sons of the Sun) and Titan Shield making Destroyers near-invulnerable to pierce (ruining the triangle per [kvoth0514]), though [thanik_] defends their siege niche. Broader issues include DLC powercreep leaving base civs behind ([solojebaconly], [rankdonator]), recastable GPs like Plenty Vault or Dwarven Mine feeling free-pop ([wrrwh]), late-game sieges being unfun/taxing ([bluewatchman], [rankdonator]), and bugs (pathing, attack-move, clumping) artificially inflating skill ceilings while tilting players like [icarus4960] and [rankdonator].

### Recommended Balance Changes
- Buff Egypt across the board: Increase Spearman HP/cost efficiency and base damage slightly; boost Camel Rider anti-archer multiplier/stats (or cost up for pop efficiency); improve Priest anti-myth DPS/speed (or shift more healing to Pharaoh) while reducing pure-gold cost; cheapen Monuments (especially later ones) and un-nerf early ones for better favor/tempo; consider Sphinx special lock-on fixes and minor MU buffs.
- Nerf Aztec outliers: Revert or heavily delay Tezcatlipoca Jaguar Riders to Mythic (or +train time/cost in Heroic); reduce Warrior Priest HP/generalist viability (keep MU focus); tone Tzitzimitl upgrade discounts and Calpulli min-range issues.
- Adjust Greeks for viability/variety: Buff Hippeus HP/armor to trade evenly with RC at equal cost (keep slower speed); restore some Amazon Archer value (e.g., minor divine damage or pop efficiency); reduce Demeter tech costs and tweak Hamadryad if overcentralizing; minor Hades compensation post-nerfs (e.g., Vault tweaks).
- Tone China flyers/eco: Nerf Qinglong special/HP or anti-air responses; slight Kuafu or favored-land adjustments; consider Pioneer range on East Wind.
- Japan tweaks: Further Raiju speed/special scrutiny; ensure Bushido doesn't enable free triple line-ups too easily.
- Atlantean fixes: Nerf Titan Shield pierce armor on Destroyers (shift bonus elsewhere like Fanatics); clarify/adjust Oracle hero costs post-tech; sky passage scaling costs if spammy.
- General: Reduce recast efficiency on eco GPs (Plenty/Dwarven Mine); improve siege vs. Architects/repair; standardize more hero anti-air consistency; address pathing/attack-move/clumping bugs as priority "balance" via responsiveness.
