### Insights into Game Balance
Players broadly agree the current state of Age of Mythology: Retold represents one of the best-balanced patches since launch, with _kiber noting that nearly every civ and god sees play in high-level events like the Athens qualifiers and that asymmetrical matchups rarely feel impossible. That said, clear outliers persist. Atlanteans (especially Gaia and Oranos) dominate discussion as perennial top picks; asdswffaqg and _iammagic highlight Gaia’s unmatched flexibility across matchups and insane tournament stats, while wrrwh and others call for rolling back her lingering eco advantages because top players still view both gods as too strong. Kronos also draws quiet praise from mathilde_moksha_333 and reconrts for deconstruction’s high-tempo value on the right maps.

Egypt stands out as the clearest underperformer. avely, reconrts, rankdonator and .teddabear paint a consistent picture: priests are gold-hungry, fragile anti-myth options that force heavy investment for mediocre results; camel riders feel like soft rather than hard counters (struggling even against upgraded Hippeus or Jarls in many tests); slingers are outright terrible; and the civ’s human roster lacks any true S-tier units after power-creep from DLC factions and AoE-heavy god powers. Monument costs and favor generation further tax Egypt’s early tempo, leaving it reliant on snowballing that no longer works. Multiple voices (including hon3ynuts and kido19) note that Egypt was left behind when base civs received catch-up buffs.

Aztecs, particularly Tezcatlipoca, generate mixed but heated takes. macdoom_ and jayyyred debate whether jaguar riders in Heroic create a one-dimensional “FH into jags” meta that warps perception of the whole kit, while twihok and others call Tez the strongest overall god at most ELOs thanks to flexible tools like Lullaby and strong classical options. China draws sharper criticism around myth units: reconrts, bigstimulus and rankdonator single out Qinglong as poorly designed—high-HP flying raid threats that are nightmarish for most civs’ limited anti-air heroes and force awkward compositions. Japanese human soldiers are repeatedly called uniformly strong (.teddabear), and Norse (especially Loki) feels tool-starved in certain matchups according to husksuppe and mathilde_moksha_333.

Broader themes include DLC civs outpacing the original roster, the lingering strength of early myth units like Centaurs (boit defends them as a healthy design target others should emulate), and QoL/friction issues (ungarrisoning, repair bugs) that indirectly affect balance perception. Several players (boit, _kiber, illuminatioimperi) stress that raw skill and adaptation still decide most games, yet acknowledge that Egypt’s structural weaknesses and a handful of overtuned myths (Qinglong, jaguar riders, certain Atlantean heroes) create frustration even when “git gud” applies.

### Recommended Balance Changes
- Revert Gaia’s 5% eco buff to bring her more in line with other top Atlanteans (suggested by wrrwh).
- Egypt priests: shift cost to 50 wood/50 gold (or 75 gold + 1 pop), raise move speed to 4.0–4.2, reduce healing output while boosting anti-myth damage; compensate by making the Pharaoh a much stronger dedicated healer (echoed by macdoom_, kido19, mathilde_moksha_333, boit, avely).
- Buff camel riders’ baseline stats or multipliers modestly so they more consistently beat the cavalry they are meant to counter without becoming late-game monsters (avely, rankdonator, nameschonvergeben, reconrts); keep Set’s gold refund in mind.
- Reduce monument costs (or un-nerf them) and/or improve Egypt favor generation so myth units become a realistic tempo option rather than a heavy investment (rankdonator, farbeyonddriven2000_17994).
- Nerf jaguar riders, especially in Heroic Age for Tezcatlipoca—higher cost, longer train time, or stat tuning so they no longer invalidate hard counters so easily (macdoom_, reconrts, mathilde_moksha_333, goodgameeveryone3915).
- Qinglong: nerf special-ability damage and/or HP/pierce armor; consider similar range or raid-effectiveness tuning for Onmoraki so flying myths are less oppressive than the Phoenix (reconrts, .teddabear, bigstimulus, ironhide99.).
- Zhuque fireball damage nerf; optional small buffs to Pioneer (East Wind +2 range) and anti-air on John Wick/Wen Zhong (reconrts).
- Minor Atlantean hero population or cost efficiency pass so they are less dominant per pop (.teddabear).
- Kuafu: remove favor cost and set to 10 pop (.teddabear).
- Prosperity god power: revert recast timer to 50 seconds and tune the percentage boost (reconrts).
- General myth-unit raid specials (Raiju, Qinglong, etc.): apply a heavy multiplier reduction versus villagers only, preserving military value while reducing “delete eco through fog” frustration (ironhide99., mathilde_moksha_333).
