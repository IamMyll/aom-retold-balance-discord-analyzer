### Insights into Game Balance

Players extensively debated Aztec early-game economy and army massing potential. Bambam noted that spearmen compositions burn food and wood rapidly (requiring 3-4 Calpulli at 125 wood each), while archer comps leverage better wood rates and starting Calpulli; he argued massing demands extra wood villagers versus a fast heroic, and that berries (often forced under pressure) are ~30% slower than hunt, creating large resource gaps (e.g., 600 food over 3 minutes, or 10+ spearmen). Hon3y found it confusing that high resource spends could yield weak armies by ~7:45 and highlighted the berry penalty. RecoN countered with screenshots showing ~strong military res at 7 minutes (including pickaxe/handaxe), questioning the need for Heroic, while BigStimulus claimed Aztecs can hit 1800 military resources by 7 minutes. Warkrool and Cajocu pushed back on sims that ignored real aggression, second-hunt risks, or god powers (e.g., Malina securing hunts), with Bambam favoring Malina into Tezcatlipoca for tempo and relegating Huitzilopochtli to water; RecoN and others agreed sheep/turkey and Fire Ceremony value matter, and that open bases or walls trade army size (Norse being an exception via low idle time). Teddabear questioned repeated berry nerfs given their inferiority to hunt and shared a gather simulator to quantify rates, while boit emphasized that apparent imbalances often stem from imperfect play rather than civs, urging self-review of replays.

A major later thread focused on myth-unit raiding versus cavalry. Ironhide, Mathilde Moksha, RecoN, and =] (v2s28) argued Raiju (especially 3 with tech at 7.3 speed), Qinglongs, Tengu, and similar units are disproportionately strong: they fly/jump over walls/forests/mountains, deliver front-loaded AoE or specials that delete villager groups before alarms (sometimes without notification), have small hitboxes allowing multi-hits on wall segments, resist towers/arrow fire better than cav, and are hard to shank or spot in TGs even near fortresses. Mathilde Moksha detailed how cav lack these traits (no fly, no multi-vill AoE burst pre-alarm, die faster to buildings) and that shanking a few cav is far more realistic than stopping Tengu/Qinglongs. Bambam compared them unfavorably to “horses with frag-grenade bows.” boit acknowledged a Raiju special-stacking bug (units launched airborne should not take further hits, so simultaneous specials on one target are unintended/abuse) but felt overall cav spam is more frustrating than myth raids in both 1v1 and TGs, and that losses often equal poor vill positioning. Ironhide proposed uniform special nerfs vs villagers (as done historically to Tengu, Butterfly, Fei beasts, Centaurs) while balancing vs military; =] agreed Fei beasts remain too strong vs low-HP targets like vills/archers and that 3-Raiju one-shots should never have left testing. Tunison and SoloJebachOnly countered that myth units and cav are fine with better map defense, though Mathilde Moksha separated balance (acceptable) from fun (several myth raiders feel unfun regardless of counterplay). Teddabear separately called for 6 range on a referenced set of units. Norse wall efficiency and Egypt early tempo (zero fixed costs) were noted as comparative advantages in defensive/raiding contexts.

### Recommended Balance Changes

- Fix the Raiju special-ability stacking bug so airborne/launched units cannot be hit by subsequent specials, preventing unintended multi-Raiju one-shots on villager groups.
- Reduce myth-unit special damage (or add a specific multiplier penalty) versus villagers for high-mobility raiders including Raiju, Qinglongs, Fei beasts, Tengu remnants, Butterfly, and similar (mirroring prior nerfs to Centaurs/Fei/etc.), while leaving anti-military performance intact.
- Lower Raiju move speed (from values around 7.3) and/or increase their train cost/time or pop to limit cheap, hard-to-spot triple raids.
- Nerf Fei beast damage or special efficacy specifically against low-HP units (villagers and archers).
- Standardize or reduce range to 6 on the units Teddabear flagged as oversized-range problems.
- Revisit berry gather rates upward slightly (or reduce further hunt advantages) so defensive/forward-pressure scenarios do not create such large (~30% or 600-food) eco gaps for civs like Aztecs that often fall back to them.
- Slightly increase Calpulli wood cost or adjust Aztec classical-age wood/food burn on mass spearmen/archer switches to better align army value with the high res investment needed for Heroic timings.
- Consider minor defensive-building (tower/fort) bonus damage or reveal range versus flying/jumping myth units so they are not effectively ignored by static defense the way cavalry is not.
- Evaluate Qinglong/Tengu-style flying mobility (wall/forest ignore) for small collison or detection adjustments to make them easier to intercept without removing the fantasy fantasy.
