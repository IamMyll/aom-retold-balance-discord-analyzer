### Insights into Game Balance

Players frequently highlight cavalry imbalances, with **Wywgf** demonstrating via gameplay footage that Greek Hippeus (even Hermes-buffed) lose straight-up fights to equivalent-cost Freyr Raiding Cavalry despite higher gold costs, calling them the slowest cav that currently loses to every other cav in the game. **Boit** enthusiastically backed Raiding Cavalry as top-tier, while **Mathilde Moksha** countered that Hippeus remain viable in high-level play since top tournament players still produce and win with them—though **Wywgf** noted Poseidon’s healing and speed make his version uniquely playable compared to Zeus/Hades variants, limiting army composition variety.

Raiding myth units drew heavy criticism for unfun villager snipes. **Ironhide** explained how three Raijus can simultaneously special-attack and delete villager groups with almost no reaction window (even behind fortresses in team games), thanks to high speed, small hitboxes, low cost, and poor tower/villager counterplay; Qinglongs share similar issues by flying over obstacles. **=]** agreed Fei Beasts remain oversized threats to low-HP units like villagers and archers, and that triple-Raiju manual specials one-shotting packs (at 7.3 speed) should never have left the test realm. **Boit** clarified that air-launched units becoming unhittable means multi-Raiju stacks are effectively bug abuse, though he finds mass cavalry (“100 horses”) more frustrating overall than myth raids in both 1v1 and TGs. In contrast, **Tunison** and **SoloJebachOnly** felt myth units and cavalry are broadly fine if players simply defend the map better, while **Mathilde Moksha** separated balance (acceptable) from fun (several myth specials feel miserable regardless of counterplay). Historical context from **Ironhide** noted prior nerfs already hit Tengu, Butterflies, Fei Beasts, and Centaurs for similar raid strength.

Secondary notes included timing of tower upgrades versus Poseidon/Odin (generally a late Classical “buy time” tool per **Farbeyonddriven2000**, **Mathilde Moksha**, **Cajocu**, and **RecoN**, but a tempo sink), Egyptian pantheon stagnation (calls from **Illuminatio imperii** and others for a fourth major god such as Geb/Aton/Maat after Sobek’s minor placement), and light Egyptian anti-myth options like Pesebeds holding up decently versus Qinglongs (**Linger**).

### Recommended Balance Changes

- Improve Hippeus straight-up combat performance versus other cavalry (retain lower speed) so Zeus/Hades can field more varied viable armies; consider minor gold-cost or stat tweaks.
- Nerf or cap stacked manual specials on high-speed raid myth units (Raiju especially) so multiple simultaneous hits cannot one-shot villager groups; reduce effectiveness of these abilities specifically versus villagers while preserving power versus military.
- Further tone down Fei Beast damage or durability versus low-HP targets (villagers/archers).
- Address Qinglong mobility/visibility so flying raids are easier to scout and intercept.
- Fix interaction bugs allowing air-launched units to be hit by multiple overlapping specials (or clarify intended behavior).
- Evaluate slight range standardization (e.g., toward 6) on certain problematic ranged/myth options raised by players.
- Longer-term: add a fourth Egyptian major god and continue polish on existing pantheon content before new civilizations.
